# Caerdon Wardrobe

Let Caerdon Wardrobe help you get your transmog closet overflowing with style ... *you fabulous thing, you*!

It puts a small icon on the items worth a second look: appearances, recipes, pets, mounts and toys you haven't learned, gear upgrades, quest items, things worth auctioning, and what's safe to sell. One glance at your bags tells you what to learn, what to mail to an alt and what to vendor.

Works on **World of Warcraft (Retail)** and **World of Warcraft: Forever**, from the same download.

## What the icons mean

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Star** | An appearance, recipe, pet, mount or toy this toon can learn. A green star is a look you already have from another item, or an ensemble with only a few pieces for this toon. Also a bag that belongs in one of your bag slots. |
| **Question mark** | This toon can't use it, but it can still be learned for your collection or by another toon. Green means one of your toons already has it. |
| **Arrow** | Gear better than what you have equipped. Yellow when Pawn says so, green for a higher item level, red when it is for another spec. The item level gain (such as +5) can be shown next to it. |
| **Quest marker** | A quest item, or anything a quest will ask you to hand in. |
| **Auction** | Sells for enough more on the auction house than at a vendor to be worth the trip. Needs Auctionator. |
| **Bag** | Nothing left for this toon to do with it: sell it. Includes food and drink you have outgrown and bags no bigger than the ones you have equipped. |
| **WoW logo** | Left over from an older expansion, to help clear out the bags. |
| **Text** | How the item binds (BoE, BoA) or the gear set it belongs to. |

Items in your bags are also marked when they can be opened, are locked, can be read, can be combined or can still be refunded.

Every icon has its own block in the settings that explains it and lets you choose where it shows.

## Where they show

Bags and bank, guild bank, vendors, the auction house, the Black Market, crafting orders, the loot window, loot rolls, quest rewards, mail, professions, the dressing room, the dungeon journal, the world map, mission rewards, the Great Vault and item conversion. Windows that only exist on Retail are simply absent on WoW Forever.

Tooltips can also say which of your toons can learn a recipe.

- **Better answers when installed:** Pawn (upgrades), Auctionator (auction prices) and QuestieDB (items for quests you have not picked up yet).

## Settings

Type `/caerdon`, or open the game's settings and pick **Caerdon Wardrobe** under AddOns.

- **Icons:** what each icon means, where it shows, and the rules behind it.
- **Display:** how the icons and text are drawn, plus the vendor, tooltip and debug options. The layout editor (`/caerdonlayout`) lets you move and resize the icon and the texts on a live example.

## Getting it

Download it from **[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/caerdon-wardrobe)**, or search for **Caerdon Wardrobe** in your addon manager, on Wago or on WoWInterface.

## Something looks wrong?

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Caerdon/CaerdonWardrobe/issues). It helps a lot to include the item's details: type `/cdebug` to open the debug frame, point it at the item, press **Copy Details** and paste the result into the issue.

I don't like slow addons and try my best to keep this one from impacting your fun. If Caerdon Wardrobe is slowing your game down, please let me know!
